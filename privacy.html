Privacy Policy

PenFinger processes camera images and drawings on your device. The developer does not receive your photos, drawings, or personal information.

Camera Usage

PenFinger uses the device camera to detect finger movement in real time and to take a still photo with your drawing when you tap the photo button. Hand tracking uses Apple’s Vision framework on your device. Photo composition also happens locally.

The app does not record video or automatically upload camera data. Captured photos are held in memory for preview until you dismiss them.

Saving and Sharing Photos

You can choose to save a captured photo to your photo library or share it using the iOS share sheet. Saving may request permission to add photos; the app does not request access to read your library. Sharing sends the photo to the app or recipient you select, whose own privacy practices apply. PenFinger does not automatically save or share captured photos.

Data Collection

PenFinger does not collect personal information, usage data, analytics, identifiers, or location data.

Third-Party Services

PenFinger does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children’s Privacy

PenFinger does not knowingly collect any data from children or users of any age.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app’s functionality changes. Any updates will be reflected on this page.
